Direct bank contribution Pay securely with PayID
PayID type: ABN 91 191 123 951

  1. Open your bank's app and choose to pay someone using PayID.
  2. Select ABN, paste the PayID, and enter any amount you choose.
  3. Check the registered account name shown by your bank before approving the payment.

If the account name looks unexpected, do not pay—email us first. If you make a payment in error, contact your bank promptly and let us know.
